# Zone of Proximal Development as AI Design Framework

## One-Line Summary
Use Vygotsky's Zone of Proximal Development (ZPD) as a design and evaluation framework for AI tutoring and adjacent-concept assistance — AI should operate in the zone between what a user can do alone and what they can do with guidance.

## Motivation
ZPD (Vygotsky 1978): the distance between what a learner can do independently and what they can do with expert guidance. AI assistants operating *below* ZPD are unnecessary; operating *above* ZPD replace rather than scaffold. The ideal is ZPD-calibrated assistance.

## Research Questions
| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| RQ1 | Can AI systems estimate a user's ZPD for a given task? |
| RQ2 | Does ZPD-calibrated assistance (scaffolding) produce better learning outcomes than uncalibrated assistance? |
| RQ3 | Does ZPD framing help evaluate AI tutoring systems? |

## Design Implications
- AI should offer hints, not answers, when user is close to the solution
- AI should provide examples when user is far from the solution
- AI should reduce scaffolding over time as user's zone expands
