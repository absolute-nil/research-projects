# Can undesirable-behavior simulators generate intervention hypotheses?

## Question
Can a simulator trained or prompted to exhibit undesirable behaviors help identify interface and LLM-behavior affordances that shift behavior toward desirable targets?

## Current Best Guess
Yes, if the simulator is scoped to a specific behavior family and all successful interventions are treated as hypotheses for human validation.

## How To Study It
- Build simulators for not fact-checking, over-reliance, confirmatory search, or low-quality feedback.
- Test affordances: source cards, uncertainty prompts, forced source inspection, agent clarification, friction, explanation style.
- Validate top interventions in [[Gricea]] with human users.
