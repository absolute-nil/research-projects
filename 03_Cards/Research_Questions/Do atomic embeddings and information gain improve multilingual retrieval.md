# Do atomic embeddings and information gain improve multilingual retrieval?

## Why It Matters
If retrieval is driven by semantic similarity alone, it may retrieve redundant majority-language evidence instead of diverse variables that improve understanding.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Decompose documents into atomic claims, entities, and narrative variables.
- Rank evidence by marginal information gain rather than only vector similarity.
- Test whether multilingual and low-resource evidence appears earlier in retrieved context.
