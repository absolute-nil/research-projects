# How do users fact-check with conversational agents?

## Why It Matters
Fact-checking through a conversational agent may reduce friction, but it can also make users outsource verification to the same system that may have introduced the problem.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Compare external verification, built-in source exploration, and agent-mediated follow-up.
- Track what users inspect, what they accept, and what they ignore.
- Measure whether conversational explanations improve or weaken independent verification.
