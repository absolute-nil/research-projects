# What are desirable and undesirable behaviors in multi-turn human-AI collaboration?

## Question
What do users, researchers, or domain experts consider desirable and undesirable when collaborating with AI across multiple turns?

## Why It Matters
Before simulating collaboration, the project needs a behavior taxonomy grounded in human judgments rather than only researcher intuition.

## Current Best Guess
Desirable collaboration includes goal alignment, clarification, repair, assumption surfacing, productive pushback, evidence grounding, and calibrated initiative. Undesirable collaboration includes premature compliance, sycophancy, goal drift, hidden assumptions, hallucinated common ground, overconfidence, passive user acceptance, and missing repair.

## How To Study It
- Survey people about concrete examples of useful and harmful multi-turn AI collaboration.
- Interview participants about tasks where they wanted the model to push back, ask questions, or take initiative.
- Use the results to revise [[Multi-turn Collaboration Behavior Taxonomy]].
