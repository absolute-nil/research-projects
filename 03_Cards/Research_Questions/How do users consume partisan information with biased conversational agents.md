# How do users consume partisan information with biased conversational agents?

## Why It Matters
A conversational agent can quietly change the distribution of arguments, source types, and uncertainty cues while the user experiences a coherent answer.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Manipulate bias intensity in retrieval and generation.
- Measure whether users notice bias, correct it, reinforce it, or disseminate it.
- Compare answer-only, cited, and debate-style outputs.
