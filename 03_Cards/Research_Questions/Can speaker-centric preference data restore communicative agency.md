# Can speaker-centric preference data restore communicative agency?

## Why It Matters
Listener-centric preference pairs reward answers that satisfy immediate user preference, while speaker-centric data could reward models for saying what helps the user reason and decide.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Collect preference data from the perspective of what the model should have communicated.
- Test verbalization of known information, presupposition rejection, clarification, and uncertainty.
- Compare downstream effects on sycophancy, grounding, multi-turn repair, and social reasoning.
