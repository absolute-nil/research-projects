# How do we distinguish completion, delegation, and collaboration?

## Why It Matters
If all multi-turn LLM use is called collaboration, we cannot tell whether the model is actually supporting shared goals, repair, and initiative.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Completion: output continuation and artifact production.
- Delegation: task execution on behalf of the user.
- Collaboration: joint management of goals, common ground, initiative, repair, and evolving plans.
