# How do we make information inferentially safe?

## Why It Matters
An output can be locally true but still lead users toward unsupported inferences, overconfident conclusions, or harmful downstream decisions.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Identify which inferences users draw after consuming an answer.
- Constrain outputs using current literature, uncertainty, and missing-evidence markers.
- Measure whether users distinguish stated facts from plausible implications.
