# What is the utility of LLM-based user simulation?

## Question
What can user simulation usefully do if it cannot perfectly or generally represent human behavior?

## Current Best Guess
Simulation is useful for cheap iteration, synthetic trace generation, study piloting, risk surfacing, and hypothesis generation. It is weak as standalone evidence unless calibrated against human studies.

## How To Study It
- Review utility claims across simulation papers.
- Classify each paper by use: evaluation, training data, policy exploration, design piloting, hypothesis generation, or social modeling.
- Compare the stated utility against validation evidence.
