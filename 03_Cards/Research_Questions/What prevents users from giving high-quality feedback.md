# What prevents users from giving high-quality feedback?

## Why It Matters
Human feedback is treated as a governance and alignment signal, but users may lack context, time, vocabulary, incentives, or trust that feedback will matter.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Study feedback quality under different interfaces, prompts, stakes, and explanation contexts.
- Compare quick ratings, structured rubrics, counterfactual edits, and deliberative feedback.
- Measure specificity, actionability, disagreement, and user burden.
