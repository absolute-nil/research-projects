# What are the boundaries of LLM-based human simulation?

## Question
What should a simulator explicitly claim and not claim?

## Current Best Guess
Every simulator should declare its behavior family, target population, task context, grounding data, simulator model, validation evidence, and known invalid uses.

## Boundary Template
| Boundary | Required statement |
|---|---|
| Behavior | What behavior is represented? |
| Population | Whose behavior is it meant to approximate? |
| Context | What task, platform, stakes, and interface? |
| Grounding | Which data, theory, or study supports it? |
| Validation | What human comparison exists? |
| Failure | Where should this not be used? |
