# How does perceived power over AI affect information consumption?

## Why It Matters
When users feel they can steer an AI system, they may trust it more, scrutinize it less, or feel more responsible for the output.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Vary control affordances such as edit, steer, regenerate, source-select, and constraint-setting.
- Measure confidence, blame, trust, verification, and downstream sharing.
- Separate actual control from perceived control.
