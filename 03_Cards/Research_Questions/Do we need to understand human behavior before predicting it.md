# Do we need to understand human behavior before predicting it?

## Question
Can simulation produce useful predictions without a causal or behavioral theory of the target behavior?

## Current Best Guess
Prediction can be useful without complete understanding, but intervention design requires at least partial theory because we need to know what affordance is supposed to change.

## How To Study It
- Compare theory-grounded simulators against purely data-driven simulators.
- Test whether each can recommend interventions that transfer to humans.
