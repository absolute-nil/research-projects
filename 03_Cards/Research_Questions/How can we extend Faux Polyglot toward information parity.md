# How can we extend Faux Polyglot toward information parity?

## Why It Matters
Faux Polyglot is not just a language failure; it is a failure to provide comparable information access across users who ask in different languages.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Build standardized metrics for information parity across languages and topics.
- Compare factual coverage, narrative coverage, cultural specificity, and uncertainty.
- Track whether model answers preserve or collapse distinct local perspectives.
