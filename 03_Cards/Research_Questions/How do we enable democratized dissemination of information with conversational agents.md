# How do we enable democratized dissemination of information with conversational agents?

## Why It Matters
Conversational agents may become public-facing information distributors. The question is whose knowledge, corrections, and priorities can enter that distribution loop.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Compare expert-only, platform-only, and community-informed information workflows.
- Study how outputs represent disagreement, uncertainty, and stakeholder knowledge.
- Build interfaces for contribution, audit, and revision.
