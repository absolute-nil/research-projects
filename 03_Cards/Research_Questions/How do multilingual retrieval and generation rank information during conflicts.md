# How do multilingual retrieval and generation rank information during conflicts?

## Why It Matters
During contested or cross-cultural events, an AI system can privilege one language, one source ecosystem, or one narrative frame while appearing neutral.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Compare retrieved and generated narratives across languages for the same information conflict.
- Separate retrieval coverage, source rank, generation framing, and citation behavior.
- Measure whether minority-language narratives are absent, delayed, mistranslated, or reframed.
