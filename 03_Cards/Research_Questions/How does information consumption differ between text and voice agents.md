# How does information consumption differ between text and voice agents?

## Why It Matters
Voice can reduce effort, increase social presence, and make verification harder, which may change trust and susceptibility during controversial information seeking.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Compare text and voice conversational search under matched content.
- Vary scarcity, source diversity, uncertainty, and environment.
- Measure belief update, source recall, cognitive load, and follow-up behavior.
