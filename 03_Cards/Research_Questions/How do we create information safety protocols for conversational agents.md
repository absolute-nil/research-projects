# How do we create information safety protocols for conversational agents?

## Why It Matters
Conversational agents need a protocol for information safety that goes beyond isolated refusal and includes inference, framing, uncertainty, source handling, and user oversight.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Define safety constraints for seeking, consumption, dissemination, and feedback.
- Test whether protocol outputs improve user understanding and oversight.
- Compare direct answers, uncertainty displays, source cards, deliberative summaries, and adapter/steer layers.
