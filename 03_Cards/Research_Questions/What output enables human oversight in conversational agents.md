# What output enables human oversight in conversational agents?

## Why It Matters
Oversight depends on what the user can inspect: sources, uncertainty, missing evidence, assumptions, tradeoffs, and alternative narratives.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Compare answer-only, source cards, evidence maps, assumption lists, and deliberative summaries.
- Measure whether users can detect unsupported claims or missing perspectives.
- Track whether oversight tools improve decisions or only increase perceived transparency.
