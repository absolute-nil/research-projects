# How can we increase the utility of human feedback?

## Why It Matters
Feedback only helps if it captures the user's intent, context, reasons, and constraints in a form that can improve systems without erasing disagreement.

## Candidate Operationalization
- Compare feedback as rating, explanation, edit, critique, preference pair, and dialogue.
- Track how much information each format preserves.
- Test whether feedback improves later model behavior or only local user satisfaction.
