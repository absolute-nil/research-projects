# How should simulations be calibrated against human studies?

## Question
What evidence is enough to say a simulator is useful for a behavior family, population, and task?

## Current Best Guess
Calibration should compare simulator and human outputs on outcome distributions, intermediate behaviors, failure modes, and subgroup errors. A simulator can be useful even if imperfect, but only within the validated boundary.

## How To Study It
- Run simulator and human users on matched Gricea tasks.
- Compare outcome metrics and interaction traces.
- Measure subgroup calibration and simulator-model robustness.
