# What Should Information Assistants Do?

## One-Line Summary
Research agenda on the appropriate role of AI information assistants: are they tutors (teaching information seeking skills), facilitators (supporting search), navigators (finding information for users), or domain teachers (teaching content)?

## Motivation
*"What should information assistants do? Is it teaching information seeking or a particular topic? How are users using AI to navigate this space?"* — This is a fundamental design philosophy question that precedes most HCI research on AI-powered search and information access.

## Role Taxonomy (Proposed)
| Role | Description | Analogy |
|------|-------------|---------|
| **Navigator** | Finds information for the user, minimizes effort | Google Maps |
| **Facilitator** | Helps user articulate and pursue their information need | Reference librarian |
| **Tutor** | Teaches information-seeking skills, meta-cognition | Teacher |
| **Domain Teacher** | Teaches the topic itself | Professor |
| **Collaborator** | Thinks through the problem jointly with the user | Peer / colleague |

## Research Questions
| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| RQ1 | What role do users expect AI information assistants to play? |
| RQ2 | What role do designers intend AI to play? |
| RQ3 | Does the role AI plays affect learning, skill atrophy, and information quality? |
| RQ4 | Is there a "right" role, or is role-switching optimal? |
