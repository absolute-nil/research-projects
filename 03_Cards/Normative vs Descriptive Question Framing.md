# Normative vs Descriptive Question Framing

## One-Line Summary
Study how asking a normative question ("Should X happen?") vs. a descriptive question ("Does X happen?") produces different AI responses — and how these differences affect user interpretation and belief.

## Motivation
Users often ask AI normative questions about contested topics without realizing it. AI systems likely respond differently to normative vs. descriptive framings (one invites opinion, the other invites fact). Understanding this gap is critical for information quality and calibration research.

## Research Questions
| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| RQ1 | Do LLMs systematically differ in their responses to normative vs. descriptive questions on the same topic? |
| RQ2 | Are users aware of this distinction when querying AI? |
| RQ3 | Does normative vs. descriptive framing change user belief update after seeing the AI response? |

## Variables
**Independent:** Question type (normative/descriptive), Topic (contested/uncontested)
**Dependent:** AI response content (opinion-laden vs. factual), User belief update, User awareness of framing

## Study Design
- **NLP study:** Systematic query experiment — same topics, normative vs. descriptive framing; code AI responses for opinion content
- **HCI study:** User experiment — measure belief change and awareness across conditions
