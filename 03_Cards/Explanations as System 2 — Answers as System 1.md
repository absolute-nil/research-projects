# Explanations as System 2, Answers as System 1

## 💡 Core Insight
LLM answers are consumed as System 1 (fast, effortless, fluent) — users accept them without critical scrutiny. Explanations and uncertainty verbalization attempt to invoke System 2 (slow, deliberate, effortful) thinking. The problem: fluency of LLM output actively undermines this, and voice makes it worse.

## 🔥 Why It Matters
This is the theoretical grounding for why uncertainty verbalization *sometimes* works but fails in voice contexts. Voice = even more System 1 (auditory processing is more automatic than reading).

## ❓ Open Questions
- Can any design intervention reliably shift users to System 2 in voice?
- Does the type of uncertainty expression matter (verbal hedge vs. explicit "I'm not sure")?
