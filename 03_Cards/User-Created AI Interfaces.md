# User-Created AI Interfaces

## One-Line Summary
Study what happens when users are allowed to design their own AI interface in JSX/low-code — what structures, controls, and interaction patterns do they invent, and what does this reveal about their mental models?

## Motivation
*"If users are allowed to create their own interface JSX, what do they create?"* — End-user programming of AI interfaces is an emerging frontier. Understanding user-designed interfaces reveals: (1) unmet needs in current AI UX, (2) user mental models of AI, (3) what controls users want but aren't given.

## Research Questions
| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| RQ1 | What interaction patterns, controls, and structures do users design when given freedom to create their own AI interface? |
| RQ2 | Do user-designed interfaces reflect mental models that differ from designer-created interfaces? |
| RQ3 | Do users who design their own interfaces perform better on tasks? |
| RQ4 | What does the diversity/similarity of user-designed interfaces tell us about universal vs. individual needs? |

## Study Design
- **Tool:** Low-code interface builder (e.g., JSX canvas, no-code blocks)
- **Task:** "Design the AI interface you'd want for [information seeking / writing / problem solving]"
- **Analysis:** Thematic analysis of designs + behavioral study comparing user-designed vs. standard interfaces
