# Is Imperfect Better Than Nothing — or Is Nothing Better Than Something?

## One-Line Summary
Research question about whether imperfect AI systems (lower accuracy, biased, poorly calibrated) help or harm marginalized and low-resource communities compared to having no AI access at all.

## Motivation
*"Is something imperfect better than nothing or is nothing better than something?"* — This is a profound equity question. A system that performs at 60% accuracy for Swahili speakers might be better than nothing, or might systematically spread misinformation in a community with no counter-narratives or fact-checking infrastructure. The answer likely depends on context.

## Research Questions
| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| RQ1 | Under what conditions does a lower-accuracy AI system produce worse outcomes than no AI system? |
| RQ2 | Does community infrastructure (fact-checkers, education, media literacy) moderate the harm from imperfect AI? |
| RQ3 | How do marginalized communities actually evaluate and use imperfect AI tools? |
