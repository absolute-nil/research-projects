# LLM-Powered Brain Tap for Enterprises

## One-Line Summary
An employee-facing "intelligent diary + chatbot" system that routes, aggregates, and acts on employee concerns, ideas, wellbeing signals, and career needs — functioning as a real-time organizational intelligence layer.

## Full Proposal Summary
Employees have ideas, concerns, and signals that get lost. A "Brain Tap" system would be a persistent AI-powered journal/chatbot that:
- Captures and categorizes employee thoughts, needs, and ideas
- Routes concerns to appropriate channels (HR, management, peers)
- Aggregates patterns for organizational insight
- Drives personalized follow-up
