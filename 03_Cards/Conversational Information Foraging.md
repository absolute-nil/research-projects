# Conversational Information Foraging

## 💡 Core Insight
Information Foraging Theory (Pirolli & Card 1999) explains how people hunt for information by following "information scent." In multi-turn conversational AI, this foraging happens through dialogue — but users navigating under confirmatory bias have their scent artificially narrowed.
