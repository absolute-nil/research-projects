# Information Safety Signals Grounded in Common Ground

## One-Line Summary
A big-picture project on forecasting the downstream effects of information on the receiver and society — and designing safety signals grounded in shared understanding (common ground).

## Motivation
*"Can we predict how information affects the trajectory of the receiver or society?"* — This is perhaps the grandest question in information science. Current content moderation is reactive; this proposes proactive safety signals based on cognitive/social common ground theory.

## Core Concepts
- **Common Ground** (Clark & Brennan 1991): the shared knowledge, beliefs, and mutual understanding between communicators
- **Information safety signal**: a measurable indicator that a piece of information is likely to cause harm to a receiver or to societal epistemic health
- **Trajectory forecasting**: modeling how information propagates and changes beliefs over time

## Research Questions
| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| RQ1 | Can we build a model that predicts how information will affect a receiver's beliefs, given their prior knowledge (common ground)? |
| RQ2 | What features of information are predictive of downstream societal harm? |
| RQ3 | Can common-ground theory ground a principled information safety signal? |
