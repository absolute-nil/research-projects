# Research Infrastructure for AI Studies

## Problem
The design space of AI systems is growing faster than our ability to run rigorous, reproducible, and comparable human-subject studies.

## Why It Matters
Without better infrastructure, AI research remains slow, fragmented, and concentrated among groups with the resources to build bespoke platforms.

## My Angle
I treat research infrastructure as part of democratic AI: better study platforms can let more researchers and communities ask better questions.

## Mechanisms
- Visual study authoring.
- Fine-grained logs and consent-aware data capture.
- Shared protocols and reproducible study designs.
- Evidence-gap maps that convert public concerns into study artifacts.
