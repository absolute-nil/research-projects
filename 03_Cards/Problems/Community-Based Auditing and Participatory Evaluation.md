# Community-Based Auditing and Participatory Evaluation

## Problem
AI evaluation is often controlled by researchers, companies, and benchmark designers rather than by the communities affected by model outputs.

## Why It Matters
Who defines evaluation decides what failure modes matter. Participatory evaluation can reveal representational harms, stakeholder dissatisfaction, and hidden assumptions in model capability labels.

## My Angle
I treat evaluation as an information governance mechanism: affected groups should help define what counts as good representation, fair portrayal, and useful model behavior.

## Mechanisms
- In-group vs out-group evaluation.
- Continuous involvement rather than one-time feedback.
- Public involvement in measurement, inference, and interpretation.
- Feedback as a way to shift power dynamics.
