# Cross-Cultural Information Access

## Problem
Conversational agents often collapse plural, multilingual, and culturally situated information into dominant-language or dominant-source summaries, limiting users' ability to access competing narratives.

## Why It Matters
Cross-cultural information access is a common-ground problem. If AI systems reinforce linguistic and geopolitical silos, they can deepen narrative asymmetry instead of helping people understand contested events.

## My Angle
I look at multilingual information access through both model behavior and stakeholder evaluation: how models retrieve and synthesize narratives, and how affected groups judge those portrayals.

## Mechanisms
- Monolingual retrieval and English fallback.
- Ground-truth mismatch in contested narratives.
- Capability labels that hide poor low-resource performance.
- Community consent and power asymmetry in language preservation.
