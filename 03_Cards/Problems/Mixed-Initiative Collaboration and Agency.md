# Mixed-Initiative Collaboration and Agency

## Problem
Current LLMs often behave as if they are completing or delegating tasks rather than collaborating with a user. They may fail to ask clarifying questions, reject presuppositions, verbalize uncertainty, or steer the conversation toward a better joint outcome.

## Why It Matters
If AI systems are going to support complex information work, they need communicative agency: the ability to negotiate goals, reveal knowledge boundaries, repair misunderstandings, and help users become better decision makers.

## My Angle
I look at agency through communication: collaboration is not only output generation, but power-sharing, joint attention, common ground, and an evolving goal.

## Mechanisms
- Listener-centric preference data may remove the communicative process.
- Speaker-centric preference data may restore communicative behavior.
- Verbalization gaps: models know more than they say.
- Framing gaps: training teaches self-talk or compliance rather than second-person collaboration.
