# Human Feedback and Power Dynamics

## Problem
High-quality human feedback is sparse, expensive, and often not collected from the people most affected by model behavior. AI feedback pipelines can become human-agnostic.

## Why It Matters
Feedback determines which behaviors models learn. If feedback is low-quality, unrepresentative, or power-imbalanced, AI systems can optimize for the wrong objectives.

## My Angle
I connect feedback to power: who gets to define the objective, who provides the signal, and whose voice is amplified or ignored?

## Mechanisms
- Feedback barriers in conversational agents.
- In-group/out-group evaluation.
- Speaker-centric vs listener-centric preference data.
- Community-based auditing as feedback infrastructure.
