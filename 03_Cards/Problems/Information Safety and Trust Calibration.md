# Information Safety and Trust Calibration

## Problem
Users need to judge information quality in AI-mediated environments, but conversational agents can blur the difference between sourced, inferred, hallucinated, biased, or contested information.

## Why It Matters
Trust calibration is central to healthy information economies. Without credible and interpretable signals, users may over-rely on fluent misinformation or under-trust useful information.

## My Angle
I study information safety as a communication and interface problem: what signals should an AI system surface, how should it verbalize uncertainty, and how should users verify claims?

## Mechanisms
- Information signals for source quality, direct quote, contentiousness, fake-news likelihood, and inference risk.
- Communication framework for how agents express uncertainty and evidence.
- UI and linguistic markers that reduce verification cost.
