# Second-Hand Thinking — Delimitation

## One-Line Summary
Conceptual and empirical project on "second-hand thinking" — the phenomenon of outsourcing cognition to AI — including how to define it, code it, and study its consequences.

## Motivation
When users accept AI outputs without independent reasoning, they engage in what could be called "second-hand thinking" — borrowing a cognitive product without doing the cognitive work. This has implications for epistemic autonomy, skill development, and susceptibility to manipulation. The challenge is delimiting this concept precisely enough to study it empirically.

## Research Questions
| # | Question |
|---|----------|
| RQ1 | How do we operationally define and measure "second-hand thinking" in AI interactions? |
| RQ2 | What factors predict whether a user engages in second-hand thinking vs. independent reasoning? |
| RQ3 | What are the downstream consequences of second-hand thinking (belief quality, skill atrophy, autonomy)? |
| RQ4 | Does modality (voice vs. text) increase second-hand thinking? |
