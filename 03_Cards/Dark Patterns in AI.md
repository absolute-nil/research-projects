# Dark Patterns in AI

## 💡 Core Insight
Voice expressiveness in LLMs (confident tone, emotional warmth, pacing) may function as an unintentional dark pattern — nudging users toward trust and compliance without explicit manipulation. Unlike classic dark patterns, these emerge from optimization for engagement, not deliberate deception.
