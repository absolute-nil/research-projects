# Make AI/Science Research Accessible to the Public

## One-Line Summary
Transform complex scientific literature on AI into public-facing material that equips end users to evaluate political and corporate claims about AI systems.

## Motivation
AI research is opaque to the public. Political and corporate actors make strong claims about AI capabilities, risks, and benefits — but citizens lack the tools to evaluate these claims. Science communication around AI is urgently needed.

## Research Questions / Project Directions
| # | Direction |
|---|-----------|
| 1 | What are the most consequential AI claims being made in public discourse? |
| 2 | What does the research actually say about those claims? |
| 3 | How can research summaries be formatted for public consumption without sacrificing accuracy? |

## Output Types
- Policy briefs
- Plain-language research summaries
- Interactive explainers
- Journalistic partnerships
