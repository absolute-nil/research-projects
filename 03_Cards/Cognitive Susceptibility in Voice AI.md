# Cognitive Susceptibility in Voice AI

## 💡 Core Insight
Cognitive susceptibility — the degree to which a user's beliefs are moved by AI output — is higher in voice modality than text due to: (1) lower cognitive load of auditory processing, (2) increased LLM expressiveness (tone, pacing, emotion), (3) passive environments where verification is impossible.
