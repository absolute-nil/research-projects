# Require at Least 2 Sources

## One-Line Summary
Experimental manipulation requiring AI users to consult at least 2 sources before forming a conclusion — encouraging source diversity and factual grounding.

## Motivation
Single-source reliance is a known driver of misinformation acceptance. Making source diversity a system requirement (not just a suggestion) may reduce over-reliance and improve epistemic quality.

## Design Notes
- System-enforced: AI won't generate final answer until user has queried 2+ distinct sources
- Compare to free-form condition on: source diversity, misinformation acceptance, satisfaction
