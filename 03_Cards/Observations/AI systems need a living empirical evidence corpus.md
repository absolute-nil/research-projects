# AI systems need a living empirical evidence corpus

## Observation
AI systems are being folded into ordinary life faster than the research community can build cumulative evidence about how they affect trust, delegation, information behavior, work, emotional support, and governance.

## Why It Might Matter
This motivates both [[Gricea]] as infrastructure and [[Democratic AI Evidence Gap Mapping]] as a research agenda. The gap is not only technical; it is institutional, because evidence is currently shaped by groups with resources to run large-scale studies.

## Evidence / Context
- HCI has long shown that interaction technologies reorganize what people notice, value, trust, and delegate.
- Public-interest AI questions need empirical evidence before years of harm accumulate.
- A living corpus could connect public concern, study design, evidence status, and policy relevance.
