# Public deliberation can become empirical research questions

## Observation
Open-ended public responses about AI can be converted into researchable questions if each question preserves the public concern, affected community, AI behavior, measurable outcome, and studyable intervention.

## Why It Might Matter
This is the bridge between democratic AI and empirical HCI/NLP work. It makes public input consequential by turning it into study designs, evidence-gap maps, and simulation priorities.

## Evidence / Context
- The draft names Global Dialogues data as a source of multilingual public responses, translated responses, demographic segments, votes, tags, and summaries.
- [[Gricea]] can instantiate study artifacts from extracted high-value RQs.
