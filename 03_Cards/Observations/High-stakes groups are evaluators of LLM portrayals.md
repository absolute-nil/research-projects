# High-stakes groups are evaluators of LLM portrayals

## Observation
For divisive topics, the people most affected by an issue may be better evaluators of whether an LLM response is representative, satisfying, or harmful than generic crowdworkers or automatic metrics.

## Why It Might Matter
This connects [[Narrative Gap]] to HCI methods. The hard question is not just whether an answer is factually correct, but whether it fairly represents contested perspectives without validating harmful or extremist frames.

## Evidence / Context
- The draft proposes collecting LLM responses to divisive-issue queries and asking in-group/out-group stakeholders to evaluate them.
- Candidate measures include truthfulness, objectivity, representativeness, diversity, relevance, cognitive load, bias, and clarity.
