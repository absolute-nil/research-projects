# Citation quantity creates credibility illusions

## Observation
Users may treat citation count as a credibility signal even when the citations do not actually support the answer.

## Why It Matters
Source cards and citations are information signals. They need evaluation for support, diversity, and relevance rather than being counted as transparency by default.
