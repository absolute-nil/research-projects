# Evaluation gives affected communities voice

## Observation
Evaluation is not only measurement. It decides whose concerns become visible, which harms become benchmarkable, and which communities can shape system improvement.

## Why It Matters
This is the bridge between public deliberation, community-based auditing, and empirical AI research.
