# Conversational search can proactively correct misinformation

## Observation
Most misinformation interventions are reactive, but conversational systems can initiate follow-up and offer recourse after a user has encountered misinformation.

## Why It Might Matter
This is a distinctive affordance of conversational search compared with static search or social feeds. It creates a research question about whether correction should happen inside the same agent relationship, and whether that increases trust, reduces trust, or changes stance.

## Evidence / Context
- The draft frames misinformation as spreading faster than factual corrections.
- Conversational systems can observe prior interaction context and intervene immediately.
- Correction may interact with confirmation bias, selective exposure, and the cry-wolf effect.
