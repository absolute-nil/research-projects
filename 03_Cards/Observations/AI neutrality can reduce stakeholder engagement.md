# AI neutrality can reduce stakeholder engagement

## Observation
LLM writing assistance can make arguments sound balanced and neutral in ways that may reduce stakeholder-specific commitment, conflict visibility, or engagement.

## Why It Matters
In contested settings, "both sides" framing can be a pragmatic failure even when the output sounds reasonable.
