# Communicative Agency

## Claim
LLMs often behave like competent listeners but weak communicative partners: they answer, continue, and comply, but they do not reliably initiate repair, reject presuppositions, surface uncertainty, or steer toward the human's actual decision quality.

## Distinctions
- Completion: the model fills in the next artifact.
- Delegation: the model performs a task on behalf of the user.
- Collaboration: human and model jointly manage goals, context, repair, and initiative.

## Research Use
This lens turns the mixed-initiative draft into a project about communication behavior, not just preference tuning.
