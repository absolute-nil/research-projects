# Information Flows

## Claim
The thesis becomes clearer if projects are organized around three recurring flows: information seeking, information consumption, and information dissemination.

## Flow Map
- Seeking -> feedback can be scaled or distorted by AI.
- Consumption -> trust can be calibrated, inflated, or misplaced.
- Dissemination -> feedback determines whose information becomes visible and credible.

## How To Use This Lens
For every project, ask:
- What information enters the system?
- Who has agency over retrieval, ranking, synthesis, correction, and refusal?
- What is the user asked to trust?
- What feedback is collected, ignored, or amplified?
- What downstream information gets redistributed?
