# Information Foraging Theory for Conversational AI

## Claim
Conversational agents change information foraging because they collapse search, synthesis, recommendation, explanation, and persuasion into a single interaction loop.

## Key Variables
- [[User Fingerprint]]: language, topic familiarity, time pressure, bias, trust, and prior beliefs.
- Information scent: citations, confidence, explanation style, source diversity, modality, and conversational cues.
- Cost structure: follow-up effort, verification effort, attention cost, and social friction.
- Patch choice: whether the user stays with an answer, asks a follow-up, checks a source, or leaves the system.

## Research Use
Use this lens to design studies that manipulate signals and observe how users move through information patches.
