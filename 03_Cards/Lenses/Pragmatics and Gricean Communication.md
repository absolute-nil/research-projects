# Pragmatics and Gricean Communication

## Claim
AI outputs should be evaluated not only by correctness but by whether they satisfy the communicative demands of a situation: relevance, quantity, quality, manner, grounding, repair, and shared purpose.

## Research Use
This lens is useful when a model output is factually plausible but pragmatically harmful: overconfident, underspecified, too neutral, too persuasive, or misaligned with the user's task.
