# Capability Labels and Taxonomies

## Claim
Broad labels like multilingual, reasoning, retrieval, or safe hide the boundaries of what systems can actually do for particular users, languages, cultures, and topics.

## Research Use
Use this lens when an evaluation needs to specify what is being claimed:
- language ability vs. topic coverage;
- retrieval availability vs. generation quality;
- cultural context vs. literal translation;
- benchmark score vs. user-facing reliability.
