# Common Ground and Deliberation

## Claim
Useful information systems do more than output correct snippets. They help participants build enough shared context to reason, disagree, repair misunderstandings, and act.

## Design Questions
- What does the system assume the user knows?
- Which assumptions are exposed, negotiated, or silently imposed?
- Does the system invite clarification and repair?
- Does personalization preserve common ground across users?
