# Power Dynamics in Human-AI Systems

## Claim
AI systems redistribute power over information: who frames the question, who ranks the sources, who gets to correct the system, and whose feedback changes future behavior.

## Design Questions
- Does the user have meaningful control, or only superficial steering?
- Whose feedback is collected and whose feedback is discarded?
- Can affected communities audit outputs that describe them?
- Does the system make disagreement legible instead of hiding it behind consensus?
