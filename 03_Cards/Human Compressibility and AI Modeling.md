# Human Compressibility and AI Modeling

## One-Line Summary
Three related conceptual ideas about the compressibility of human knowledge and behavior as a foundation for understanding AI capabilities.

## Three Connected Seeds

### 1. All Information Is Just a Repeat
*"All information is just a repeat / how much pattern is actually out there"*
- AI capability may emerge from redundancy and pattern repetition in human-generated information
- Question: how compressible is human knowledge? How much genuine novelty exists?

### 2. Compress Knowledge to Atomic Facts
*"If we compress the data into absolute facts such that all regurgitation is pruned, then how much data or information do we actually have?"*
- Thought experiment: if you removed all restatements, paraphrases, and redundancies from human text, what would remain?
- Relates to minimum description length (MDL) and knowledge compression
- May inform why LLMs work — they exploit redundancy

### 3. Humans Are More Predictable Than We Think
*"It's easy to mimic humans because we are not as unpredictable as we think we are"*
- Humans have a lower entropy than we believe
- Generative models succeed because human behavior has high regularity
