# Overarching Thesis

## One-Sentence Thesis
Conversational AI is becoming the dominant human-information interface, and my research studies how it reshapes information seeking, consumption, dissemination, and governance so that we can design systems that support common ground, calibrated trust, and equitable information access.

## Core Transition
From information at your fingertips to knowledge at your fingertips.

## Thesis Tracks
1. [[Track I — Human Behavior with AI]]: understanding and shaping how people seek, consume, trust, verify, and disseminate information through AI.
2. [[Track II — Conversational Agent Behavior in Complex Contexts]]: understanding and shaping how AI systems retrieve, synthesize, rank, and present information in pluralistic, multilingual, multimodal, and non-ground-truth contexts.
3. [[Track III — Democratized Information Governance]]: building tools, protocols, and participatory frameworks for safer, more accountable information flows.
