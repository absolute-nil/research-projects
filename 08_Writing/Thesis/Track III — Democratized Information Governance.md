# Track III — Democratized Information Governance

## Purpose
Build tools, protocols, and participatory workflows that let communities, researchers, and institutions audit AI-mediated information flows instead of relying only on platform-owned evaluation pipelines.

## Core Lens
This track connects [[Community-Based Auditing and Participatory Evaluation]], [[Information Safety Protocol]], [[Public deliberation can become empirical research questions]], and [[Research Infrastructure for AI Studies]].

## Anchor Questions
- [[How do we create information safety protocols for conversational agents]]
- [[How can we increase the utility of human feedback]]
- [[How do we make information inferentially safe]]
- [[How do we enable democratized dissemination of information with conversational agents]]
- [[How do we scale manipulation of conversational agents for large-scale studies]]
- [[What output enables human oversight in conversational agents]]

## Evidence Needed
- A protocol for translating public concerns into measurable AI study questions.
- Auditing infrastructure that supports multiple communities, topics, and contexts.
- Clear privacy, consent, and governance defaults for study data.
