# Track I — Human Behavior with AI

## Purpose
Understand and shape how people seek, consume, trust, verify, revise, and disseminate information when the interface is a conversational AI system rather than a document, feed, search page, or social platform.

## Core Lens
This track treats user behavior as a product of [[Information Flows]], [[User Fingerprint]], [[Information Foraging Theory for Conversational AI]], and [[Power Dynamics in Human-AI Systems]].

## Anchor Questions
- [[How does information consumption differ between text and voice agents]]
- [[How do users consume partisan information with biased conversational agents]]
- [[How do users fact-check with conversational agents]]
- [[How does perceived power over AI affect information consumption]]
- [[What prevents users from giving high-quality feedback]]

## Evidence Needed
- Behavioral studies that compare modalities, environments, and interventions.
- Measures for trust calibration, over-reliance, susceptibility, and verification.
- Evidence that intervention designs change behavior without creating false certainty.
