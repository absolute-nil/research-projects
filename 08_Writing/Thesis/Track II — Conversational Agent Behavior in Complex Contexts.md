# Track II — Conversational Agent Behavior in Complex Contexts

## Purpose
Study how AI systems retrieve, rank, synthesize, refuse, clarify, hedge, and adapt in settings where there is no single clean ground truth: multilingual conflicts, contested public narratives, scarce information, multimodal search, and mixed-initiative collaboration.

## Core Lens
This track connects [[Communicative Agency]], [[Pragmatics and Gricean Communication]], [[Common Ground and Deliberation]], and [[Capability Labels and Taxonomies]].

## Anchor Questions
- [[How do multilingual retrieval and generation rank information during conflicts]]
- [[How can we extend Faux Polyglot toward information parity]]
- [[Do atomic embeddings and information gain improve multilingual retrieval]]
- [[How do we output information to different personas while maintaining common ground]]
- [[How do we distinguish completion delegation and collaboration]]
- [[Can speaker-centric preference data restore communicative agency]]

## Evidence Needed
- Benchmarks and audits that separate language ability, topic familiarity, retrieval coverage, and cultural context.
- Interaction evaluations that test clarification, presupposition rejection, grounding, and repair.
- Model behavior taxonomies that state what a system can and cannot do.
