# Nikhil Fellowships Research Statement

## Current Framing
AI is shifting the human-information interface from search pages and documents toward conversational synthesized answers. My research studies how this changes information seeking, consumption, dissemination, and governance, then designs systems that make AI-mediated information flows more equitable, accountable, and useful.

## Three-Track Structure
1. [[Track I — Human Behavior with AI]]: how people seek, consume, trust, verify, and disseminate information through AI.
2. [[Track II — Conversational Agent Behavior in Complex Contexts]]: how agents retrieve, rank, synthesize, clarify, and communicate in multilingual, contested, and multimodal contexts.
3. [[Track III — Democratized Information Governance]]: tools and protocols for public-interest auditing, participatory evaluation, and research infrastructure.
