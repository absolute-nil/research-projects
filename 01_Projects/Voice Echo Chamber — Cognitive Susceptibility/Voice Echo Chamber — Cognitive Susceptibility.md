# Project: Voice Echo Chamber — Cognitive Susceptibility

## 🎯 Thesis Fit
Demand side — how users are influenced by AI-mediated information. This is the first paper to isolate voice modality as an amplifier of the Generative Echo Chamber effect.

## 🧵 One-Paragraph Pitch
Users of text-based LLMs already over-rely on AI outputs, leading to downstream misinformation propagation. Voice modality adds three compounding factors: decreased cognitive load, increased LLM expressiveness, and passive interaction environments (driving, exercising). This study measures whether cognitive susceptibility increases in voice vs. text conversational search, whether environment (active vs. passive) and topic familiarity moderate this, and whether existing solutions (uncertainty verbalization, citations) transfer to voice.

## ❓ Research Questions
| # | RQ | Method |
|---|----|----|
| RQ1 | How does cognitive susceptibility change in voice vs. text modality? | 2×2 experiment |
| RQ2 | Does topic familiarity change the effect? | Moderator analysis |
| RQ3 | Does a passive/limited-resource environment exacerbate susceptibility? | Moderator analysis |
| RQ4 | What existing solutions are deployable in voice and what is their effect? | Condition comparison |
| RQ5 | What new solutions are needed? | Design implications |
