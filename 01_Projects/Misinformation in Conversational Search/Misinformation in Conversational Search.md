# Misinformation in Conversational Search

## Pitch
This project studies how users fact-check polarizing information when the information environment is mediated by a conversational AI agent. It also studies whether conversational systems can correct prior misinformation proactively and how users respond to those corrections.

## Current Question
When conversational search systems provide consonant, dissonant, neutral, or corrected information, how do users change their fact-checking behavior, follow-up questions, trust, and stance?

## Research Questions
| ID | Question | Method | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| RQ1.1 | Do users fact-check at different rates under consonant, dissonant, and neutral information? | experiment | seed |
| RQ1.2 | Does placement of misinformation change fact-checking frequency? | experiment | seed |
| RQ1.3 | Does frequency of misinformation change fact-checking frequency? | experiment | seed |
| RQ2.1 | Do users follow up after an agent presents corrected information? | interaction log analysis | seed |
| RQ2.2 | What kinds of follow-up questions do users ask after correction? | qualitative / behavioral coding | seed |
| RQ2.3 | Do users change stance after being presented with corrected information? | pre/post survey | seed |
| RQ2.4 | Does correction increase or decrease trust in the system? | trust measurement | seed |
