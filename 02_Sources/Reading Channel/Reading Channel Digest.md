## Recurring Themes
- Interfaces should surface affordances and constraints instead of requiring users to learn hidden prompt languages.
- Evaluation design shapes what communities get to name as harms or capabilities.
- Citations, neutrality, and source count are information signals, not neutral metadata.
- Multilingual capability needs bounded claims: language ability, topic coverage, cultural specificity, and user-facing reliability are different.
