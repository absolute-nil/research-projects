# Finetuning LLMs for Human Behavior Prediction in Social Science Experiments

## One-Sentence Takeaway
Fine-tuning on individual-level responses from many past social-science experiments can improve prediction of human response distributions, including generalization to unseen studies or conditions.

## Why I Care
This paper is the strongest counterpoint to the "simulation is a gimmick" stance: there is a plausible path where simulations are grounded in large-scale empirical behavior data.

## Use In My Work
Use it to argue that simulation should be theory- and data-grounded, then scoped to specific behavioral families rather than presented as a general human proxy.
