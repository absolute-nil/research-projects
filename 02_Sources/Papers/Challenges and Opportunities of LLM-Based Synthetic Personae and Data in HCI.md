# Challenges and Opportunities of LLM-Based Synthetic Personae and Data in HCI

## One-Sentence Takeaway
Synthetic personae and data are promising for HCI piloting, design, and speculation, but raise questions about authenticity, ethics, and requirements for future HCI research.

## Why I Care
This places the MSR project squarely inside HCI: simulation should help design and study preparation, not silently replace people.
