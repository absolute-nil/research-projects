# How Reliable is Your Simulator?

## One-Sentence Takeaway
Conversational recommender simulators can appear strong because of data leakage, dependence on conversational history, and weak control over simulator outputs.

## Why I Care
This is a practical warning for Gricea: synthetic user traces must be leakage-checked, logged, and separated from human validation data.
