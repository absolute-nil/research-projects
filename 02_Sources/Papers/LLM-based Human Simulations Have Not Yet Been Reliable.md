# LLM-based Human Simulations Have Not Yet Been Reliable

## One-Sentence Takeaway
The paper argues that current LLM-human simulations are limited both by model behavior and by simulation design choices, so broad human-proxy claims need tighter scoping and validation.

## Why I Care
This is the cleanest citation for the MSR framing: user simulation should not be treated as final evidence unless it is calibrated, scoped, and validated against human data.
