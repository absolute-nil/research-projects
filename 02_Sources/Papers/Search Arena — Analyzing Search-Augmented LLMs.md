# Search Arena — Analyzing Search-Augmented LLMs

## Why It Belongs Here
The Slack discussion highlighted a trust-calibration issue: users may prefer responses with more citations even when the citations do not support the claim.

## Use In My Work
- Connects to [[Citation quantity creates credibility illusions]].
- Useful for study design around source cards, citation sufficiency, and answer credibility.
- Useful dataset candidate for conversational search behavior and model training, with caution about limited multilingual coverage.
