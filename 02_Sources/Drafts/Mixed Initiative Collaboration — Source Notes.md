# Mixed Initiative Collaboration — Source Notes

## Core Argument
The draft argues that LLMs lack agency in collaboration because current preference data is largely listener-centric. Models are optimized to produce answers users prefer, not to act as communicative partners that manage shared goals, repair misunderstandings, or protect the quality of the user's eventual decision.

## Important Distinctions
- Completion: the model continues or produces an artifact.
- Delegation: the model performs a task for the user.
- Collaboration: the model and human jointly manage goals, constraints, common ground, initiative, and repair.

## Hypotheses To Preserve
- LLMs may know relevant information but fail to verbalize it when the user does not ask in the right way.
- LLMs may fail to reject presuppositions because compliance is rewarded.
- Speaker-centric preference data could reward communicative behavior that helps the human make a better decision.

## Candidate Evaluation Behaviors
- Clarifying questions.
- Presupposition rejection.
- Uncertainty communication.
- Grounding-gap repair.
- Multi-turn recovery.
- Social reasoning and collaboration quality.
